A map, not a diagnosis — save this if you keep saying "I don't know, I just feel off."

Three things can arrive at once: a tired body, a feeling with no name, a thought on a loop. And you have one word for all three.

This tradition maps a person as seven layers — physical, etheric, astral, mental, causal, buddhic, atmic. You don't have to believe any of it. Borrow it as vocabulary.

Today, three times, one line each:
— what's in the body
— what's in the feelings
— what thought keeps circling
Three words each. No more.

By the end of the week one layer will be louder than the rest. That's the one actually asking for attention.

Not medicine, not therapy. One tradition's lens, not a fact — if something here doesn't fit, it doesn't apply to you.
